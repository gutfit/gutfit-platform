About
Dijana Spajic
Founder & lead trainer
Welcome! I am Dijana Spajic, co-founder of Gutfit, fitness trainer, and evidence-based nutritionist. I specialize in helping women and men optimise their gut biome, reduce body fat, strengthen the immune system while becoming physically and mentally resilient. Over the past eight years, I've perfected a life-changing, sustainable process that revolves around gut health. This approach not only addresses various health-related concerns but can also be tailored to achieve additional unique goals, such as building or maintaining muscle, balancing nutrition, and improving sleep quality.
From a young age, I’ve had a deep interest in health, inspired by my active and health-conscious parents. However, at 13, I witnessed my mother’s health decline due to a condition that led to medication destroying her immune system and causing significant weight gain. This experience opened my eyes to the shortcomings of conventional medicine, which often offers temporary fixes instead of addressing root causes. I realized that there are aspects of our health we can control, and this insight sparked my desire to understand what it truly means to be "healthy."
I became mindful of the importance of being whole and healthy, which led me to take a deep dive into holistic wellness. I firmly believe in taking control of health through a well-rounded approach. This journey led me to understand that food is the foundation. Nothing will solve the problem until we address the problem with food. Let food be thy medicine.
My purpose is to help you tap into your best self—your strongest, most confident self. I believe everyone has this potential within them. With the right guidance, we can all step into self-empowerment. Nothing brings me more fulfillment than seeing my clients discover their power and become the best versions of themselves. Let me help you get there.
Dr. Guillermo Wilches
Founder & psychotherapist
I am Dr. Guillermo Wilches (Dr. G.).  I am a father, husband, academic, entrepreneur, and scientist, among other things; but mainly I am a passionate human who chooses to live, each day, a balanced, healthy lifestyle. Today I am present!
I have a degree in Clinical Psychology from Konrad Lorenz University, and a Ph.D. in Management Sciences from the University of Waterloo. In the last couple of decades, I had to undertake a fight against a very complex illness that has impacted my life in many different aspects. However, it also gave me the opportunity to start a new and better pathway! The most arduous time was in 2023 when I had a spinal cord injury, my body collapsed. I lost 45 kg in 12 months, and I couldn’t even feed myself, at times. My immune system became severely compromised, and I developed numerous illnesses. My muscles were devastated and felt like marmalade—no control, no sensation, only pain. After consulting many specialists in Canada, the USA, and Colombia. I had more unknowns, and unanswered questions, However, the answers to these questions so critical for my life, would never come. My only hope, a miracle!
Dijana and I developed a unique program using holistic focused protocols that boosted my immune system, strengthened my muscles, and got my body-mind connection back. I “gutfit”, lost fat, and still today, remain mindful. Throughout my years of clinical psychology practice, I witnessed behavioural change happening slowly, often inconsistently. Traditional treatments and interventions are incomplete. They do not involve a holistic approach to a better life. Using my whole body, mind and spirit approach to psychology, I created behavioural change that healed my mental trauma I was experiencing which also tied into my physical struggles. Today I am mindful, healthier, and stronger than yesterday! Today I have purpose!!!
