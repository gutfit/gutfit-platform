## 🎯 Core Program Components

### **Movement Protocols**

#### **Daily Movement Practice (30 minutes)**

- **Warm-up (5 minutes)**: Dynamic stretching and movement preparation
- **Main Workout (20 minutes)**: Varied intensity based on program phase
- **Cool-down (5 minutes)**: Static stretching and recovery

#### **Weekly Movement Schedule**

- **Monday**: Strength Training (Upper Body Focus)
- **Tuesday**: Cardiovascular Health (Moderate Intensity)
- **Wednesday**: Active Recovery (Gentle Movement and Stretching)
- **Thursday**: Strength Training (Lower Body Focus)
- **Friday**: High-Intensity Interval Training
- **Saturday**: Movement Variety (Sports, Dance, Outdoor Activities)
- **Sunday**: Rest and Reflection

#### **Movement Progression Principles**

- **Progressive Overload**: Gradually increasing intensity and duration
- **Variety**: Preventing boredom and plateaus through movement diversity
- **Recovery**: Balancing challenge with adequate rest and recovery
- **Enjoyment**: Focusing on activities that bring joy and satisfaction

### **Nutritional Protocols**

#### **Daily Eating Schedule**

- **Morning (7:00 AM)**: Hydration and light breakfast
- **Mid-Morning (10:00 AM)**: Nutrient-dense snack
- **Lunch (12:30 PM)**: Balanced meal with protein, vegetables, and healthy fats
- **Afternoon (3:30 PM)**: Energy-boosting snack
- **Dinner (6:30 PM)**: Lighter meal focused on vegetables and lean protein
- **Evening (8:00 PM)**: Herbal tea and optional light snack

#### **Nutritional Principles**

- **Whole Foods Focus**: Emphasizing unprocessed, natural foods
- **Balanced Macronutrients**: Proper ratios of proteins, carbohydrates, and fats
- **Micronutrient Density**: Maximizing vitamin and mineral intake
- **Hydration Priority**: Adequate water intake throughout the day

#### **Meal Preparation Strategies**

- **Batch Cooking**: Preparing multiple meals at once for efficiency
- **Ingredient Prep**: Washing and chopping vegetables in advance
- **Smart Storage**: Proper food storage for freshness and convenience
- **Quick Assembly**: Having components ready for fast meal assembly

### **Lifestyle Protocols**

#### **Daily Routine Structure**

- **Morning (6:00 AM)**: Hydration, light movement, and intention setting
- **Midday (12:00 PM)**: Mindful eating and brief movement break
- **Afternoon (3:00 PM)**: Energy-boosting activity and mental break
- **Evening (6:00 PM)**: Relaxation and reflection activities
- **Night (9:00 PM)**: Wind-down routine and sleep preparation

#### **Stress Management Techniques**

- **Breathing Exercises**: Daily practice for stress reduction
- **Movement Breaks**: Short movement sessions throughout the day
- **Mindfulness Moments**: Brief periods of present-moment awareness
- **Gratitude Practice**: Daily acknowledgment of positive aspects of life
